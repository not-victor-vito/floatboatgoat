Goat Watch

Tap a word to claim it, tap again to un-claim. Claim all three and the round is yours.

Scores save automatically on this device.

Players

Rename anyone, or add up to four players. Scores are kept when you rename.

The first two players cannot be removed. Removing the last player asks you to confirm, because it deletes their scores. Adding or removing a player also clears the undo button.
